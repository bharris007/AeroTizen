AeroTizen Calendar: Privacy policy

Last updated 21 November 2026

AeroTizen Calendar is a personal app made by Bryan Harris for his household. It shows family calendar events on the household's Samsung TV (the AeroTizen launcher). It is not a commercial service.

What the app accesses

  • When you sign in with Google, the app asks for read-only access to your Google Calendar (calendar.readonly) and your email address, so it can show which account a calendar came from.
  • It never creates, changes or deletes anything in your calendars and has no access to your email, contacts, files or anything else in your Google account.

How it is used

Calendar data is used only to show your events on the TV you paired. Events are fetched when the TV refreshes (a few days at a time) and passed straight to the TV; the relay does not keep a copy of your events.

What is stored

  • The relay stores your Google refresh token and the IDs, names and colours of the calendars you picked. It is encrypted (AES-256-GCM) and can only be used together with the paired TV's secret.
  • The TV stores the calendar names and colours and a short-term cache of upcoming events.
  • Pairing codes expire after 15 minutes and are deleted within a day.

Sharing

Nothing is sold, shared, or used for advertising, analytics or AI training. Data goes only between Google, the relay (hosted on Vercel) and your TV.

AeroTizen Calendar's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting your data

  • On the TV, open Settings → Calendars and choose Remove account. This deletes the stored token from the relay and revokes it at Google.
  • Or revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. After that the app can no longer read your calendar.
